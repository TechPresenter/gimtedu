# Recruiter logos: sources and trademark notes

These are the official logos of the companies that recruit GIMT students. `database/seed/95_recruiter_logos.php` links
them to `companies.logo`. Every file was downloaded once from Wikimedia (`upload.wikimedia.org`) and is bundled here,
so nothing is hotlinked. Downloaded 2026-10-12.

**Trademarks.** Every logo is a registered trademark of its company. The Wikimedia licence (public domain, CC0 or
CC BY-SA) covers only the copyright in the image. It grants no trademark rights. "Fair use" files are non-free logos
hosted on English Wikipedia. GIMT uses all of these logos only to name the companies that recruit its students
(nominative use). Do not alter them, recolour them or use them to suggest endorsement. Remove a logo if its company
asks.

**How the files were processed.** Each logo was cropped tightly to its visible artwork on a transparent background.
SVGs were cleaned up: editor metadata, comments, unused ids, scripts and external references were removed, and
numbers were rounded. Each SVG's viewBox was set to the artwork and its height to 40. Some sources were rasterised to
PNG and fitted within 400×160 px (each file is 60 KB or less):

- ICICI and Asian Paints, because their SVGs embed bitmap images.
- Reliance Retail, because its SVG uses a live font.
- Flipkart, because its SVG is 135 KB.

The colours, shapes and proportions were not changed.

## Sources considered but not used

- **Simple Icons** (`tools/.cache/simple-icons`, CC0). It has marks for tata, tcs, infosys, wipro, hcl, accenture,
  hdfcbank, icicibank, axisbank, unilever and suzuki. They are single-colour glyphs, and several show only a symbol
  without the name. The full-colour official lockups from Wikimedia were used instead.
- **Fictional demo companies** get no logo and keep the text wordmark fallback: NovaByte Technologies, FinEdge
  Payments, GreenLeaf Agritech, Brightpath Learning, Skyline Infra Projects.
